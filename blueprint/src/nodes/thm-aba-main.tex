\begin{theorem}[Refinement and correctness of ABA]
  \label{thm:aba-main}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.refines}\lean{PLTS.ABA.main}\leanok
  \uses{thm:aba-netflat, thm:aba-exploded-atd, thm:aba-substitution, thm:aba-flatspec-refines, thm:aba-coresim, thm:aba-spec-safe}
  Every trace distribution achievable by the deployed protocol is achievable by
  the ABA specification, and hence every positive-probability trace of the
  deployed protocol satisfies Validity and Agreement.
  The four steps of the chain enter as four inclusions chained by transitivity
  of set inclusion; transitivity of probabilistic forward simulation is not
  used.
  The composed simulation is additionally provided, by
  Theorem~\ref{thm:forward-trans}, along the composite of the four relations:
  the graph of the regrouping, the pointwise round substitution, the graph of
  the deflation, and the core relation.\lean{PLTS.ABA.simComposed}
  All three are machine-checked axiom-clean, documented by pinned axiom guards.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{thm:aba-netflat, thm:aba-substitution, thm:aba-flatspec-refines, thm:aba-coresim, thm:aba-spec-safe, thm:trace-support, thm:forward-sound, thm:forward-trans}
  The two conclusions are those of Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-netflat}, assembled
  from the substitution inclusion of Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-substitution}, the
  deflation inclusion of Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-flatspec-refines}, and soundness
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:forward-sound}) applied to
  Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-coresim}.
  For the composed simulation, apply transitivity of probabilistic forward
  simulation (Theorem~\ref{thm:forward-trans}) to the four simulations
  themselves rather than to their soundness inclusions.
  The two routes are independent: the inclusion never invokes transitivity of
  simulation, so the headline does not rest on it.
\end{proof}
