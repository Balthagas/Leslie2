\begin{theorem}[The deflation onto the analysis-side composition]
  \label{thm:aba-flatspec-refines}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.deflCoreA}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecDefl}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecForward}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecSim}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpec_refines}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-flatspec, def:aba-hybrids, def:aba-core, def:strongfunctional, def:achievable, thm:forward-sound}
  For all parameters $P$,
  \[
    \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{flatSpec}(P)) \;\subseteq\;
    \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{hybridSpec}(P)).
  \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-flatspec, def:aba-hybrids, def:strongfunctional, def:achievable, thm:forward-sound}
  The two systems hold the same four layers under different partitions.
  The round specifications and the coin oracle occupy the same coordinate on
  both sides.
  The two ABA-side factors are the monolithic $\mathrm{CoreState}$ of
  Definition~\ref{def:aba-core} cut along the layer boundary, and the deflation
  $\mathrm{flatSpecDefl}$ is the inverse cut: the round loops supply the control
  records $procs$ and the receipt rows $decidedRecv$, the ABA-side network
  supplies the sent pools $decidedSent$ and the corrupted set $F$.
  It carries the initial state to the initial state.

  Each row of $\mathrm{flatSpec}(P)$ writes one round-loop node and one network
  slot, and read through the deflation the pair is the matching one-row update
  of the monolithic state.
  A quorum count over a round loop's own receipt rows is the monolithic count
  at the deflated state, the receipt rows being the round loops'.
  The guard $b \in dpool(id)$ on a return is the monolithic guard on
  $decidedSent$.
  The corrupted set is the network's on one side and the monolithic $F$ on the
  other, and the two $\mathrm{corrupt}$ transforms share the guard
  $k \notin F \wedge |F| < f$, so the $\mathrm{fail}$ rows correspond without a
  side condition.
  The hidden rendezvous of the deployment-shaped side --- the DECIDED relay and
  delivery, the fused round advance, the call loop and the five drives --- are
  each the monolithic row they assemble to, already relabelled to $\tau$ on
  both sides; the two hiding frames of Definition~\ref{def:aba-flatspec} are
  applied in the same order as the single frame of
  Definition~\ref{def:aba-hybrids} together with the identification of the
  rendezvous.
  The coin resolution is the same pushforward of $\mathrm{wccPMF}$ on both
  sides.

  So $\mathrm{flatSpecDefl}$ commutes with steps, and
  Lemma~\ref{def:strongfunctional} gives a probabilistic forward simulation
  along its graph.
  Soundness (Theorem~\ref{thm:forward-sound}) turns it into the inclusion.
\end{proof}
