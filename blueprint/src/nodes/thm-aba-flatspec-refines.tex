\begin{theorem}[The deflation onto the analysis-side composition]
  \label{thm:aba-flatspec-refines}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.deflCoreA}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecDefl}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecForward}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecSim}\lean{PLTS.ABA.flatSpec_refines}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-flatspec, def:aba-hybrids, def:aba-core, def:strongfunctional, def:achievable, thm:forward-sound}
  For all parameters $P$,
  \[
    \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{flatSpec}(P)) \;\subseteq\;
    \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{hybridSpec}(P)).
  \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-flatspec, def:aba-hybrids, def:strongfunctional, def:achievable, thm:forward-sound}
  The two systems hold the same four layers under different partitions, and the round specifications and the coin oracle occupy the same coordinate on both sides.
  The deflation \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.flatSpecDefl} is the inverse cut: it assembles the $n$ round loops and the ABA-side network into the monolithic $\mathrm{CoreState}$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-core} and carries the initial state to the initial state.
  Read through it, each row of $\mathrm{flatSpec}(P)$ is the matching one-row update of the monolithic state, the two $\mathrm{fail}$ rows share the guard $k \notin F \wedge |F| < f$ and so correspond without a side condition, the hidden rendezvous are the monolithic rows they assemble to, and the two hiding frames of Definition~\ref{def:aba-flatspec} are applied in the order of the single frame of Definition~\ref{def:aba-hybrids}.
  So the deflation commutes with steps, Lemma~\ref{def:strongfunctional} gives a probabilistic forward simulation along its graph, and soundness (Theorem~\ref{thm:forward-sound}) turns it into the inclusion.
\end{proof}
