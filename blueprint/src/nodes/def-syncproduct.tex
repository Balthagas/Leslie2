\begin{definition}[The full-synchronisation product]
  \label{def:syncproduct}
  \lean{PLTS.System.syncProduct}\lean{PLTS.System.syncProduct_isLTS}\leanok
  \uses{def:idle-family}
  For $sys_i : \mathrm{System}\,(State_i)\,\mathrm{Label}$ over a finite index type, the product $\mathrm{syncProduct}(sys)$ steps every component simultaneously on a visible label, with the joint successor the independent product, and on $\tau$ steps exactly one.
  Ownership of a label is carried by the components and not by the operator, which stays a single conjunction over the family: a component idles on the labels it does not own (Definition~\ref{def:idle-family}), so a label owned by exactly two components moves those two alone, and a label no component offers is blocked.
  A product of LTS components is an LTS.
  Full construction: \leandecl{PLTS.System.syncProduct}.
\end{definition}
