\begin{definition}[The exploded system]
  \label{def:aba-exploded}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.CoreProcStepN}\lean{PLTS.ABA.coreProcN}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ANetState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ANetState.corrupt}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ANetStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.aNet}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ExpState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.explodedPre}\lean{PLTS.ABA.explodedGroup}\lean{PLTS.ABA.exploded}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbcasub, def:aba-procnet, def:aba-netadv, def:aba-core-vocab, def:relabel, def:syncproduct, def:process-algebra}
  The deployed protocol cut along its layer boundaries: four factors over the
  extended alphabet $\mathrm{NLab}(n)$.

  The first is the family $\mathrm{gbcaSide}(P)$ of round subsystems of
  Definition~\ref{def:aba-gbcasub}.

  The second is the round loop of each process, $\mathrm{coreProcN}(P,j)$ over
  the node $\mathrm{CoreNodeN}(n)$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-procnet}, the $n$
  of them under the product of Definition~\ref{def:syncproduct}.
  Its twenty-seven rows are the core-slice half of the rows of
  Definition~\ref{def:aba-procnet}: the external call and the return, the two
  sub-protocol handshakes, the DECIDED relay and delivery, the fused round
  advance, and an idle row wherever another process is named or the row's whole
  content is a stage write.
  The six multicast levels and the stage delivery leave no row at all, their
  events being internal to a round subsystem.
  These programs are not round-filtered: a round loop answers every round's
  $\mathrm{callG}$, its own as a participant and every other process's as a
  bystander.

  The third is $\mathrm{aNet}(P)$, what is left of the network adversary once
  the round-tagged pools have gone to the round fabrics.
  Its state $\mathrm{ANetState}(n)$ is the DECIDED pools $dpool(j)$ (D12$'$)
  and the corrupted set $F$.
  Its seventeen rows are the DECIDED layer --- the relay, the delivery, the
  publication carried by the fused round advance, the injection
  $\mathrm{byzD}$, and the guard $b \in dpool(id)$ on $\mathrm{retABA}$ --- the
  budget-guarded corruption row $\mathrm{fail}$ (D1), the authorisation
  $k \in F$ of each of the five Byzantine drives (D11), and idle rows
  elsewhere.

  The fourth is the coin oracle, joined through the partial map
  $\mathrm{wccPull}$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-netadv}.

  The composition is that of Definition~\ref{def:aba-netadv}, factor for
  factor:
  \[
    \mathrm{explodedPre}(P) \;=\;
      \mathrm{gbcaSide}(P) \parallel \bigl(\mathrm{syncProduct}_{j}\ \mathrm{coreProcN}(P,j)
        \parallel (\mathrm{aNet}(P) \parallel \mathrm{wccLift}(P))\bigr),
  \]
  \[
    \mathrm{explodedGroup}(P) \;=\; \mathrm{relabel}\bigl(
      \mathrm{abstract}_{\mathrm{netEvtLabels}}(\mathrm{explodedPre}(P))\bigr),
  \]
  \[
    \mathrm{exploded}(P) \;=\;
      \mathrm{abstract}_H\bigl(\mathrm{explodedGroup}(P)\bigr).
  \]
  The graded-agreement family occupies the left argument of the outer parallel
  composition, which is the refinable slot.
\end{definition}
