\begin{definition}[The coin specification]
  \label{def:aba-wcc-spec}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.WCC.Step}\lean{PLTS.ABA.WCC.specFamily}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-labels, def:aba-params, def:idle-family}
  Transition System 3, as a round-indexed instance and the $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family of Definition~\ref{def:idle-family} over it: a call quorum, then one resolution of the coin.
  The resolution follows the distribution of Definition~\ref{def:aba-params}, the one both coin resolutions of the development share, and it is the only rule of this system that is not Dirac.
  Its fourth outcome $\dagger$, of mass $\failmass$, delivers to no process and so freezes the round it resolves (D17).
  Full rule table: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.WCC.specFamily}.
\end{definition}
