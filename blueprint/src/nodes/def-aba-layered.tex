\begin{definition}[The layered system]
  \label{def:aba-layered}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.CoreProcStepN}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.coreProcN}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.ANetState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.ANetState.corrupt}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.ANetStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.aNet}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.LayeredState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.layeredPre}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.layeredGroup}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Layer.layered}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbcasub, def:aba-procnet, def:aba-netadv, def:aba-deployed, def:aba-core-vocab, def:relabel, def:syncproduct, def:process-algebra}
  The deployed protocol cut along its layer boundaries into four factors over the extended alphabet $\mathrm{NLab}(n)$: the family $\mathrm{gbcaSide}(P)$ of round subsystems, the round loop $\mathrm{coreProcN}(P,j)$ of each process, the ABA-side network $\mathrm{aNet}(P)$ holding the DECIDED pools (D12$'$) and the corrupted set with its budget, and the coin oracle joined through $\mathrm{wccPull}$.
  \[
    \mathrm{layeredPre}(P) \;=\;
      \mathrm{gbcaSide}(P) \parallel \bigl(\mathrm{syncProduct}_{j}\ \mathrm{coreProcN}(P,j)
        \parallel (\mathrm{aNet}(P) \parallel \mathrm{wccLift}(P))\bigr)
  \]
  runs through the pipeline of Definition~\ref{def:aba-deployed} factor for factor, giving $\mathrm{layeredGroup}(P)$ inside the inner frame and $\mathrm{layered}(P)$ inside the outer.
  A round loop is not round-filtered: it answers every round's $\mathrm{callG}$, its own as a participant and every other process's as a bystander.
  A round subsystem is a factor of the composite at every moment, so the graded-agreement coordinate of a layered state is $\mathbb{N} \to \mathrm{ImplState}(n)$: every round keeps its stage records and its fabric for the whole run, whichever round each process is in.
  That retained per-round memory is specification-side state, held by no process (D20).
  The graded-agreement family occupies the left argument of the outer parallel composition, which is the refinable slot.
  Full construction: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Layer.layered}, over \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Layer.coreProcN} and \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Layer.aNet}.
\end{definition}
