\begin{definition}[The ABA core]
  \label{def:aba-core}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.core}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-labels, def:aba-core-vocab}
  The round loop regrouped as one PLTS over the whole system state $\mathrm{CoreState}$, the analysis form of \algoref{alg:aba-core}{the ABA implementation}.
  Each process runs the five-phase handshake cycle of Definition~\ref{def:aba-core-vocab}, carrying an estimate adopted from the round's graded outcome or, when that outcome leaves it unset, from the round's coin; the bookkeeping between the coin return and the next call is fused into the $\mathrm{retW}$ step (D10), which multicasts $\langle\mathrm{DECIDED},b\rangle$ when the round's grade was $A\,b$.
  A DECIDED gossip layer pools the multicasts per process (D12$'$), relays on $f{+}1$ distinct receipts, and gates $\mathrm{retABA}\,id\,b$ on $n - f$ distinct senders of $b$ together with $b$ in $id$'s own sent pool; corrupted processes additionally carry Byzantine call- and return-driver rules at each handshake (D11).
  Full rule table: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.core}.
\end{definition}
