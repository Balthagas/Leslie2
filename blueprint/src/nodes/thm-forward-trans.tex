\begin{theorem}[Transitivity of probabilistic forward simulation]
  \label{thm:forward-trans}
  \lean{PLTS.ProbabilisticForwardSimulation.trans}\leanok
  \uses{def:forwardsim}
  If $sys_C$ is forward-simulated by $sys_B$ along $R_{BC}$ and $sys_B$ is forward-simulated by $sys_A$ along $R_{AB}$, then $sys_C$ is forward-simulated by $sys_A$ along the composite relation $\operatorname{compRel} R_{BC}\ R_{AB}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:forwardsim, def:weakstep, lem:bridge}
  The composite relation holds of $s_C$ and $\mu_A$ when some intermediate $\mu_B$ satisfies $R_{BC}$ against $s_C$ and simulates $\mu_A$ under $R_{AB}$.
  For the initial condition, bind the intermediate initial distribution against the constant abstract one.
  For the step condition, match the concrete step on the $sys_B$ side, which yields a weak transition out of $\mu_B$, and lift that weak transition along $R_{AB}$ to a weak transition of $sys_A$.
  That lift is the crux: it promotes a simulation of single steps to a simulation of the $\tau^\ast \cdot l \cdot \tau^\ast$ segments a weak transition is built from, and it supplies the coupling witnessing the composite relation on the successors.
\end{proof}
