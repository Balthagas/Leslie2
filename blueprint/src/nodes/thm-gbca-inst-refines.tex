\begin{theorem}[GBCA instance refinement]
  \label{thm:gbca-inst-refines}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.implRefines}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-impl, def:aba-subspecs}
  The GBCA implementation instance is forward-simulated, instance by instance, by the GBCA specification instance.
  The relation reads the specification's $call$, $ret$ and $F$ off the implementation state and certifies its $dead$ and $grade$ by receipt patterns: a \emph{kill certificate} for every member of $dead$, and an $n{-}f$ SEAL quorum for each side of the grade latch.
  Kills are fired on demand: a return met by a specification state whose $dead$ lacks the bit that return needs excluded is answered by the two-step weak burst $\mathrm{bindUnset};\mathrm{ret}$, and every return row --- $C$-returns included --- does the same case split on $dead$, however many returns preceded it.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-impl, def:aba-subspecs, def:forwardsim}
  The relation is checked constructor by constructor, and the work is in the certificates it carries for the two fields the protocol does not store.
  A member $b$ of $dead$ is certified either by an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{ECHO}\,(1-b)$ receipt quorum or by an $n{-}f$ wall of processes each corrupted or committed by its write-once $\mathit{sentVote}$ to a payload other than $b$; each side of the grade latch is certified by an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{SEAL}$ quorum at its own payload.
  All of these are $F$-blind and monotone in the delivered sets, in the write-once send slots and in $F$, so they survive every step and every $\mathrm{fail}$, and the relation bounds $dead$ from above only.
  Two quorum intersections carry the argument: either kill certificate makes an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{VOTE}\,b$ quorum impossible forever, that quorum being the sole gateway to grade-$\ge 1$ evidence for $b$; and an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{SEAL}\,v$ quorum cannot coexist with an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{SEAL}\,\bot$ quorum, which is A/C exclusivity.
  A return of $v$ met by a state with $1-v \notin dead$ harvests from its own guard an honest $\mathrm{BIND}\,v$ sender, whose $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{VOTE}\,v$ quorum both certifies $1-v$ dead and refutes any certificate for $v$, and refines to an $\mathrm{INPUT}\,v$ quorum supplying the quorum guard and the $f{+}1$ $\mathrm{SuppOK}$ count of the kill (D8, D15); a $C$-return names no bit, so its evidence certifies some bit instead, by a split on whether any honest process ever voted a bit, and which certified bit is killed never matters, since a certified bit carries no return evidence in any extension.
  Every such count passes through the invariant clause $input\_supp$, that an honest multicast of $\langle\mathrm{INPUT},b\rangle$ is by a genuine holder of $b$ or already certifies $f{+}1$ distinct processes each a genuine holder of $b$ or corrupted, and transporting one to the specification side is a rewrite along $call = \mathit{input}$ and the aligned corrupted sets.
  A corruption row is answered by the specification's own $\mathrm{fail}$, and every delivery, relay and protocol-send row by a specification stutter.
\end{proof}
