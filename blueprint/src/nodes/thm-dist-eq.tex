\begin{theorem}[The distribution-monad lift preserves trace distributions]
  \label{thm:dist-eq}
  \lean{PLTS.dist_traceProb_eq}\leanok
  \uses{def:achievable, def:dist}
  $\operatorname{ATD}(sys) = \operatorname{ATD}(\mathcal{D}(sys))$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{lem:map}
  The easy inclusion (\leandeclnamed{dist_traceProb_subset}{PLTS.dist_traceProb_subset}) is Lemma~\ref{lem:map} for the Dirac embedding $s \mapsto \delta_s$, under which a step $s \xrightarrow{l} \mu$ lifts to the hyper-step $\delta_s \xrightarrow{l} \delta_\mu$.
  The hard inclusion (\leandeclnamed{dist_traceProb_superset}{PLTS.dist_traceProb_superset}) flattens a $\mathcal{D}(sys)$-execution back to an ordinary one by the lowering \leandeclnamed{lower}{PLTS.ProbabilisticExecution.lower}.
  At a concrete history the lowering forms the trace-cone belief \leandeclnamed{beliefTC}{PLTS.ProbabilisticExecution.beliefTC}, the posterior over those $\mathcal{D}(sys)$-histories carrying the same full label list, weighted by the mass each of them places on the current state.
  It then emits the corresponding average of hyper-steps, split into per-state $sys$-steps by \leandeclnamed{distHyperKernel}{PLTS.ProbabilisticExecution.distHyperKernel}.
  Conditioning on the full label list rather than on the external trace keeps the normaliser finite, it being a level sum, and makes the step a genuine posterior.
  The Dirac-init clause of $\operatorname{ATD}$ is what makes the reconstruction sound: a mixture initial distribution over $\operatorname{PMF} State$ need not be realised by any single $sys$-execution.
\end{proof}
