\begin{theorem}[The round subsystem simulation]
  \label{thm:aba-subsim}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.subDefl}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.sub_projects}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.gPull}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.liftedSpecG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.Rsub}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.subSim}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.subSim_init}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.subSim_failAct}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbcasub, def:aba-gbca-impl, def:aba-subspecs, thm:gbca-inst-refines, def:forwardsim}
  For all parameters $P$ and every round $r$, the round subsystem $\mathrm{sub}(P,r)$ is forward-simulated by the round-$r$ GBCA specification instance, read over the subsystem's interface.
  The relation is the instance relation of Theorem~\ref{thm:gbca-inst-refines}, read on the deflated state.
  It holds at the initial states and survives the corruption broadcast.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbcasub, def:aba-gbca-impl, def:aba-subspecs, thm:gbca-inst-refines, def:forwardsim, def:weakstep}
  The argument runs in two legs through the monolithic instance of Definition~\ref{def:aba-gbca-impl} and reproves nothing of Theorem~\ref{thm:gbca-inst-refines}.
  The first leg is the strong functional bijection \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.GSub.subDefl}, which reads the programs' stage records and inbox rows and the fabric's pools and corrupted set as the four fields of the instance state; every transition of the subsystem is a transition of the instance at the deflated state, one step for one step.
  The second leg is the partial map \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.GSub.gPull}, which identifies the drives and the call loop of round $r$ with the specification labels they stand for, and along which the specification instance is read back by the construction $\mathrm{mapIdle}$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-netadv}.
  Composing the legs, a step of the subsystem on $l$ is a step of the instance on $\mathrm{gPull}(l)$, which Theorem~\ref{thm:gbca-inst-refines} answers by a weak transition transported segment by segment along the section of $\mathrm{gPull}$ that returns $l$; the specification is input-enabled on the driven call and the driven return, so that answer needs no rule of its own (D11).
  The initial states are related under the deflation, and the corruption broadcast preserves the relation, the fabric's corrupted set being the instance's and the two budget guards the same proposition (D1).
\end{proof}
