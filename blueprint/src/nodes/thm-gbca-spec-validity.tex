\begin{theorem}[Validity of the GBCA specification, safety half]
  \label{thm:gbca-spec-validity}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.specInst_validity}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-subspecs, thm:trace-support}
  Fix a bit $v$ and a trace in the support of an achievable trace distribution of the round-$r$ GBCA specification instance.
  Call the trace \emph{unanimous at $v$} when every round-$r$ call of the trace carries $v$, unless the calling process is corrupted somewhere along the trace.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.UnanimousInput}\leanok{}
  Corruption is the trace-level notion of Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-spec-safe}, non-membership in the fold of the corruption transform over the trace's $\mathrm{fail}$ labels, so a $\mathrm{fail}$ the budget refuses corrupts nobody.\lean{PLTS.ABA.NeverCorrupted}\leanok{}
  On a trace unanimous at $v$, every round-$r$ return of the trace hands out $v$.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ValidityTrace}\leanok{}
  The conclusion quantifies over all returners, corrupted ones included, and it rules out the $C$-return outright, $C$ handing out no bit.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.specInst_no_retC}\leanok{}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-subspecs, thm:trace-support}
  The instance carries one bookkeeping invariant: every pending input has a $\mathrm{callG}$ event in the label history, and $F$ is the fold of that history's $\mathrm{fail}$ labels.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.CallInv}\leanok{}
  Both conjuncts are read off the rules, and the filter that produces the trace keeps every $\mathrm{callG}$ and every $\mathrm{fail}$ label, so both transfer from the history to the trace.
  At any state of the execution every supporter of the dissenting bit $1-v$ is corrupted at some stage of the trace, a caller of $1-v$ by unanimity and a member of $F$ by the fold; the stages form a chain, so the whole set sits inside a single stage, which the corruption budget caps at $f$.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.supp_le_of_unanimous}\leanok{}
  Every $f{+}1$ guard at that bit is therefore unreachable, so the kill at $v$ never fires and $v$ is alive at every state.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.dead_notMem_of_unanimous}\leanok{}
  A value-bearing return of $w$ from such a state demands $1-w \in dead$, so $1-w \neq v$ and $w = v$, while a $C$-return demands $f{+}1$ support at both bits and so does not fire.\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.retG_value_of_unanimous}\leanok{}
  Theorem~\ref{thm:trace-support} supplies the genuine execution behind the trace and pulls each return label back to a step of it.
\end{proof}
