\begin{definition}[The process-algebra operators]
  \label{def:process-algebra}
  \lean{PLTS.System.parallel}\lean{PLTS.System.interleave}\lean{PLTS.System.abstract}\leanok
  \uses{def:system}
  Three operators build compound systems over a shared label type.
  \emph{Parallel composition} $sys_1 \parallel sys_2$ has state space $State_1 \times State_2$; it synchronises on a visible label, with successor the independent product of the two component successors, and on $\tau$ moves exactly one component.
  \emph{Interleaving} $\operatorname{interleave} sys$ of an indexed family has state space $\prod_i State_i$ and moves exactly one component per step, at any label.
  \emph{Abstraction} $sys.\operatorname{abstract} L$ keeps the states and the initial state and relabels every $L$-labelled transition to $\tau$, leaving transitions on labels outside $L$ as they are.
  Full constructions: \leandecl{PLTS.System.parallel}, \leandecl{PLTS.System.interleave} and \leandecl{PLTS.System.abstract}.
\end{definition}
