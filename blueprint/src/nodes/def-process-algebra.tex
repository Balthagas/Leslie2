\begin{definition}[The process-algebra operators]
  \label{def:process-algebra}
  \lean{PLTS.System.parallel}\lean{PLTS.System.interleave}\lean{PLTS.System.abstract}\leanok
  \uses{def:system}
  Three operators build compound systems over a shared label type.

  \emph{Parallel composition} $sys_1 \parallel sys_2$ has state space
  $State_1 \times State_2$ and initial state the pair of initial states. It
  synchronises on visible labels and interleaves on $\tau$: a step labelled
  $l \neq \tau$ requires both components to step on $l$, with successor the
  independent product $\mu_1 \otimes \mu_2$; a step labelled $\tau$ moves
  exactly one component and holds the other fixed.

  \emph{Interleaving} $\operatorname{interleave} sys$ of an indexed family has
  state space $\prod_i State_i$ and moves exactly one component per step, at
  any label: $s \xrightarrow{l} \mu$ when some $sys_i$ has
  $s_i \xrightarrow{l} \mu_i$ and $\mu$ is the independent product of $\mu_i$
  with the Diracs on the unchanged components.

  \emph{Abstraction} $sys.\operatorname{abstract} L$ leaves states and initial
  state untouched and relabels the alphabet: every $L$-labelled transition
  becomes a $\tau$-transition, and transitions on labels outside $L$ are kept
  as they are.
\end{definition}
