\begin{definition}[Parameters and the coin]
  \label{def:aba-params}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.Params}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Params.wccPMF}\leanok
  The parameters of the case study are $n$ processes, at most $f$ corrupted with $3f < n$, coin goodness $\varepsilon$ and coin failure mass $\failmass$ subject to $2\varepsilon + \failmass \le 1$, together with the one coin distribution they induce, which both coin resolutions of the development are read off, that of Transition System~1 through the map that forgets the bit (D21): \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Params} and \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Params.wccPMF}.
\end{definition}
